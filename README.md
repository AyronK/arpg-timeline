## Features

- **Dashboard** - current and upcoming seasons for every major aRPG, with live countdowns, progress bars, reveal-stream timers and Twitch live indicators
- **Your games** - pick the games on your dashboard or start from a featured set, switch between all, community and news views, or browse everything on a timeline
- **Game pages** - overview, season history, news, guides and stats for each game
- **Season stats** - average season length, seasons per year and live Steam player counts
- **News** - official updates from Steam and Blizzard in one place
- **Search** - find any game, season or article with Ctrl/⌘+K
- **Calendar sync** - [subscribe](https://www.arpg-timeline.com/calendar) to all games or just one; new seasons appear in your calendar automatically
- **Discord bot** - new league and season starts show up as events in your server ([add to your server](https://www.arpg-timeline.com/discord-bot), [source on GitHub](https://github.com/AyronK/arpg-timeline-discord-bot))
- **Widgets** - embeddable countdown and season widgets for websites and OBS overlays
- **API for patrons** - REST endpoints with an [OpenAPI spec](https://www.arpg-timeline.com/docs/api), available to Patreon supporters
